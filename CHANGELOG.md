# 1.0.0 — 2026-10-19

Initial release of WhereYouAt Universal, based on WhereYouAt 1.0.0 by samfoxy.

- Uses the client’s localized GameFontNormal font instead of a fixed FRIZQT__.TTF file.
- Preserves zone display for group leaders and individual players in the LFG browser.
- Adds English documentation, localized summaries, and English/Russian screenshots.
- Tested in English and Russian on Forever beta 1.60.1.70450. Other languages are not yet verified.
- Original MIT license retained unchanged.

Disable the original WhereYouAt before enabling Universal.
